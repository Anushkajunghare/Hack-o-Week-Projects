Trip 01 · Mountains

Mountains
Adventure

There's a particular kind of silence you only find above 3,000 metres — where the air thins, the trees surrender to rock, and the sky feels impossibly close. My trek through the Himalayas was exactly that.

Each morning I woke to frost-laced tents and a horizon painted in amber. The trails wound through rhododendron forests, past frozen waterfalls, and over wind-carved ridgelines that made every tired step feel worth it. On day six, I reached the summit camp — and cried. No reason. Every reason.

🏔 Himalayan Range 📅 14 Days 🥾 Trekking
Trip 02 · Beach

Beach
Vacation

After months of grey city winters, the Maldives hit me like a warm embrace. Crystal-clear lagoons in a dozen shades of blue, powdery white sand that squeaked underfoot, and evenings that melted into electric sunsets.

I spent mornings snorkelling through coral gardens alive with parrotfish and sea turtles, afternoons reading in a hammock, and nights watching bioluminescent plankton wash ashore like spilled starlight. Some places don't just rest the body — they restore the soul.

🏝 Maldives 📅 10 Days 🤿 Snorkelling
Trip 03 · City

City
Exploration

Tokyo is a city that operates on a frequency all its own. Neon-drenched alleys give way to centuries-old temples around the next corner; convenience stores serve food that rivals Michelin-starred restaurants; bullet trains run to the second.

I wandered Yanaka's old wooden lanes at dawn, got happily lost in Shinjuku's labyrinthine underground, and watched the city glow from a skyscraper observatory at midnight. Every street corner was a story, every meal a revelation. Tokyo doesn't ask you to understand it — just to experience it fully.

🗼 Tokyo, Japan 📅 12 Days 🚇 Urban Explorer

About Me

Hey, I'm Maya 👋

I'm a 28-year-old freelance writer and photographer with an incurable case of wanderlust. I've visited 34 countries — but who's counting? (I am. Very closely.)

Travel Diaries started as a personal journal and grew into something bigger: a space to share honest stories, practical tips, and the unexpected moments that make every journey unforgettable. I believe travel isn't just about the destination — it's about becoming a slightly different person each time you return home.

When I'm not on the road, you'll find me in a cosy café, editing photos and plotting my next escape. ☕
